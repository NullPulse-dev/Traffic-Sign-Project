System Pipeline Architecture

The complete flow from image capture to decision support, mirroring the research methodology.

1
IMAGE ACQUISITION
Capture road scenes using onboard vehicle cameras at 30 FPS. The raw frames are fed into the preprocessing module.
2
PREPROCESSING
Resizing to 224x224, normalization of pixel values (0-1), and data augmentation (rotation, zoom, brightness).
3
YOLOv8 DETECTION
Localizing traffic signs and drawing bounding boxes around the region of interest (ROI) to isolate the sign from the background.
4
CNN CLASSIFICATION
Classifying the isolated ROI into one of 43 categories. The system then maps the class to a driving action (e.g., slowing down for a speed limit).

Traffic Sign Recognition System for Autonomous Vehicle Assistant

A B.Sc. Computer Science Research Project • Nasarawa State University, Keffi

Abstract

This study presents the design and implementation of a Traffic Sign Recognition System for Autonomous System Assistant with the aim of improving road safety and enhancing the decision-making capability of autonomous driving systems through automatic detection and classification of traffic signs. The study investigates the problem of inaccurate or delayed recognition of traffic signs caused by poor lighting conditions, weather variations, occlusion, faded signs, and human error, which reduce the reliability and safety of autonomous vehicle assistant systems. A quantitative system development methodology was adopted, involving the collection of traffic sign image datasets, image preprocessing, feature extraction, and the training and evaluation of a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) model implemented using Python and OpenCV. The system performance was evaluated using standard machine learning metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score. The findings indicate that the proposed system achieves high classification accuracy, fast recognition speed, and improved robustness under varying environmental conditions, thereby providing reliable real-time traffic sign recognition for autonomous system assistant.

Methodology

  • Dataset: GTSRB (50,000+ images).
  • Detection: YOLOv8 Architecture.
  • Classification: CNN.
  • Tools: Python, OpenCV, TensorFlow.
